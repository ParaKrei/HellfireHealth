# COMPATIBILITY STUFF:

**Hellfire Health comes with unique compatibility for certain mods to ensure that both meld together as much as possible!
This mod also comes packed with a fix to NiGHTS stages to prevent soft-locks when all players die.**

| Mod Name | Link | What happens |
| - | - | - |
| **Silverhorn** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/silverhorn.5936/ | If allowed, your partner bot has it's own health, and swapping characters also swaps health. |
| **Doomguy** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/doomguy.5880/ | If allowed, Doomguy will use Hellfire Health as a shield, requiring the health rings to reach one before his own health/armor takes damage. |
| **Takis** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/takis-the-fox-dynamite-demo.5937/ | If allowed, Takis will use Hellfire Health just like Doomguy. |
| **N64 Mario/Luigi** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/n64-mario-bros.3251/ | If allowed AND if "n64power" is off, Mario and Luigi will use Hellfire Health instead of their own health system. |
| **Echoes & Abyss** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/stephchars-v4-echoes-of-the-abyss-update.706/ | If allowed, the health rings will protect Echoes and Abyss, tanking the damage until one health ring is left. If Echoes has any health rings while Abyss isn't around, she will still be protected by the health rings. Abyss will only damage nearby enemies upon taking a hit IF there's only ONE health ring left. |
| **2011x** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/2011x-sonic-exe.8757/ | If allowed, the health rings will protect you against X. Failing escapes will no longer insta-kill you if you have health, but X will NOT hesitate to grab you when enraged no matter what ring count you're at, if you have one health ring left. |
| **Orange Demon** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/orange-demon.5243/ | If allowed, the health rings will protect you against the demon, preventing instant death on touch... as long as you have health. |
| **BattleMod** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/srb2-battle-multiplayer-gameplay-mod.171/ ***OR*** https://mb.srb2.org/addons/battle-mod-jab-edition.5458/ | Blocks, shields, and hurt messages function properly (This is important, due to how Hellfire Health and BattleMod handles damage)! |
| **Metroid Vanguard** | https://mb.srb2.org/addons/metroid-vanguard-samus-aran-in-srb2-v1-4.2666/ | Non-Samus players now have a better chance of survival against the mod's unique forms of damage, as the health rings will take the damage for you! (Hint: If something ever latches onto you, spin dash or move really fast)! |
| **Legacy Cream** | **NOT PUBLICLY RELEASED YET; CAN BE FOUND ON SAOSHI'S MODDED PLANET** | If allowed, Cream will use Hellfire Health just like Doomguy and Takis. |
